## 3. Comprehensive Summary of Regulatory & Actuarial Findings

### The 2026 Post-Subsidy "Price Cliff"

1.  **Gross vs. Net Premium Increases:** While the Pennsylvania Insurance Department (PID) approved a statewide weighted gross rate increase of **21.5%** in the individual market and **12.7%** in the small group market for 2026, the net impact on enrollees receiving enhanced tax credits was drastically higher. With the expiration of federal EPTCs on December 31, 2025, net monthly premiums doubled, increasing by an **average of 102%** for subsidized Pennie enrollees.
2.  **Disenrollment and Coverage Loss:**
    *   During Open Enrollment 2026, **85,000 enrollees terminated coverage** (~18% of Pennie's active base).
    *   By June 2026, total post-Open Enrollment drop-off exceeded **125,000 enrollees**, bringing active enrollment down by over 10% from its 2025 peak of 497,000.
    *   Survey data indicates that the vast majority of disenrollled individuals became uninsured rather than securing alternative employer or private coverage.
3.  **Disproportionate Rural Impact:** Proportional disenrollments were significantly higher in rural counties, which comprised **15 of the top 20 counties** for coverage terminations. Lower population density and limited carrier competition amplified cost shocks in these areas.
4.  **Coverage Downgrades:** Over **33,000 enrollees shifted down to Bronze-tier plans** (a 30% increase in Bronze enrollment), trading lower monthly premiums for significantly higher deductibles and out-of-pocket liabilities.

### The 2027 Outlook

1.  **Continued Rate Pressure:** For Plan Year 2027, the PID approved a statewide weighted average rate increase of **16%** for individual marketplace plans and **10%+** for small group plans across 13 participating insurers.
2.  **Carrier Rate Variations (2027 Approved):**
    *   **Ambetter Health of PA:** +35.1%
    *   **Keystone Health Plan Central:** +33.4%
    *   **QCC Insurance Company (IBC PPO):** +21.3%
    *   **Highmark Benefits Group:** +19.7%
    *   **Jefferson Health Plans (HMO / PPO):** +18.4% / +18.8%
    *   **Keystone Health Plan East (HBC HMO):** +16.0% (Requested +14.7%)
    *   **Capital Advantage Assurance:** +14.8%
    *   **UPMC Health Plan / Health Network:** +13.5% / +9.7%
    *   **Geisinger Health Plan / Quality Options:** +8.6% / +10.7%
    *   **Oscar Health Plan of PA:** +1.4%
3.  **State Mitigation Gap:** Pennsylvania established a framework for a **State Health Insurance Affordability Program** under Act 54 of 2024 (Fiscal Code). Designed to leverage $50M in state funding to reduce premiums by 9%–12% and restore coverage for ~20,000 Pennsylvanians, the program remained unfunded as of late 2026.
